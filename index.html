Notation Label Description Category
Capability What the organization is able to do. Use to describe business abilities independent of people, processes, or systems. Strategy
Course of Action A strategy, approach, or plan to achieve a goal. Use for policies, strategies, and improvement initiatives. Strategy
Resource An asset required by a capability. Use for people, money, information, equipment, or technology. Strategy
Value Stream Sequence of activities that delivers value to a customer or stakeholder. Strategy
Business Actor A person, team, department, or organization that performs behavior. Think "who does it". Business
Business Collaboration Multiple actors working together toward a shared purpose. Business
Business Event Something that happens and triggers or affects business behavior. Business
Business Function Business behavior grouped by required skills or resources. Business
Business Interaction Behavior performed jointly by multiple business actors. Business
Business Interface Access point through which business services are offered. Business
Business Object Business information used or produced by business behavior. Business
Business Process Sequence of business activities producing a business outcome. Business
Business Role A set of responsibilities assigned to actors. Business
Business Service Business functionality delivered to customers or other parties. Business
Contract Formal agreement governing products or services. Business
Product A package of services and value offered to customers. Business
Representation A readable form of information such as a report, form, or dashboard. Business
Application Collaboration Multiple applications working together. Application
Application Component A deployable software system or application. Application
Application Event Something that happens within an application. Application
Application Function Internal automated behavior performed by an application. Application
Application Interaction Behavior jointly performed by multiple applications. Application
Application Interface An API, UI, endpoint, or integration point exposed by an application. Application
Application Process An automated workflow executed by software. Application
Application Service Functionality exposed by an application to users or other applications. Application
Data Object Information processed by applications. Application
Artifact A physical or digital implementation of software or data. Technology
Communication Network Infrastructure enabling communication. Technology
Device Physical computing equipment such as servers, laptops, or routers. Technology
Material Physical matter used or produced by the enterprise. Technology
Node A computational environment hosting software. Technology

ArchiMate Rule of Thumb

Layer Main Question Typical Elements
Strategy Why are we doing this? Capability, Resource, Value Stream, Course of Action
Motivation What are we trying to achieve? Driver, Goal, Outcome, Requirement, Principle, Stakeholder
Business Who does it and how? Actor, Role, Process, Function, Service, Product
Application Which software supports it? Application Component, Service, Interface, Data Object
Technology Which infrastructure runs it? Node, Device, System Software, Technology Service
Implementation & Migration How do we get there? Work Package, Deliverable, Plateau, Gap
